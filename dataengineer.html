About Forbes Advisor

Forbes Advisor is a consumer-focused initiative under the Forbes Marketplace umbrella that provides expert-written insights, reviews, news, and guidance on personal finance, health, business, and everyday life decisions.

The platform helps consumers make informed decisions through high-quality research, analysis, and expert recommendations.

Job Overview

Data Engineer - L2 Remote Python SQL Marketing Data

Forbes Advisor is looking for a Data Engineer (L2) with strong experience in Python, SQL, and data ingestion pipelines. The role focuses on building marketing analytics datasets and supporting campaign reporting through reliable data infrastructure.

Responsibilities

Data Engineering & Pipelines

  • Build and maintain data ingestion pipelines from APIs and external sources.
  • Write efficient Python and SQL for data processing.
  • Support ETL/ELT workflows and microservices.
  • Troubleshoot pipeline failures and improve performance.

Marketing Data Support

  • Ingest and model data from Meta Ads and marketing platforms.
  • Create datasets for campaign reporting and lead funnel analysis.
  • Understand campaign structures and performance metrics.
  • Support conversion tracking and marketing attribution.

Business Collaboration

  • Work closely with analytics and marketing teams.
  • Support lead quality and campaign performance analysis.
  • Translate business requirements into datasets and queries.

Data Quality & Maintenance

  • Implement validation checks and monitoring.
  • Resolve data quality issues.
  • Maintain technical documentation.

Required Skills & Qualifications

Core Technical Skills

  • Strong Python programming skills.
  • Advanced SQL knowledge.
  • API integration and data ingestion experience.
  • Workflow orchestration tools (Airflow or similar).
  • Cloud data warehouse experience (BigQuery, etc.).

Ad Platform Exposure

  • Basic understanding of Meta Ads platform.
  • Marketing metrics (CTR, CPC, CPA, Conversions).
  • Lead generation and funnel tracking concepts.
  • Interest in learning AdTech systems.

Soft Skills

  • Strong problem-solving ability.
  • Debugging and troubleshooting skills.
  • Cross-functional collaboration.
  • Excellent communication.

Good to Have

  • Google Ads experience.
  • dbt or similar transformation frameworks.
  • GA4 and event tracking knowledge.
  • Marketing analytics experience.

What Success Looks Like

  • Reliable execution of data pipelines.
  • Accurate and timely delivery of marketing datasets.
  • Strong understanding of marketing data.
  • Effective collaboration with stakeholders.
