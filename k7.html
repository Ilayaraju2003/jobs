Overview

K12 Techno Services Pvt. Ltd. is a leading education management services company that currently operates and manages more than 60 schools across various cities under the brand name Orchids The International School. In addition to this, the company manages multiple educational brands under its umbrella.

The company provides end-to-end educational management services through partnerships with school-owning trusts. K12 Techno Services effectively manages and operates the entire school ecosystem, including academics, administration, operations, marketing, and communications.

As pioneers in bringing professional management to K-12 education in India, the organization focuses on expanding quality education through standardization and innovation.

The company is backed by renowned investors and partners, including Sequoia Capital and Navneet Publications.
